Our Mission

Making Property Simple for Every Motswana

The Botswana property market has historically been difficult to navigate — scattered listings, unverified agents, and a lack of transparent pricing. HomeFinder was founded in 2022 to change that.

We built a platform where buyers can trust what they see, agents can grow their business, and sellers can reach serious buyers — all powered by technology designed for the Botswana market.

From Gaborone's growing suburbs to the tourism market in Maun and Kasane, we're building the infrastructure that Botswana's property industry deserves.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

🔍
Transparency
Every listing shows accurate pricing, real photos, and verified agent credentials. We don't allow bait-and-switch or misleading descriptions.
🤝
Trust
All agents are REIB-licensed and manually verified. Buyers can message any agent with confidence, knowing they're dealing with a real professional.
🌱
Local Impact
We reinvest in Botswana's property ecosystem — supporting local agents, funding first-home education, and expanding to underserved areas.
📱
Accessibility
Built as a PWA so it works on any device, even on slower connections. Property search shouldn't require a fast internet connection or expensive smartphone.
⚡
Speed & Simplicity
Finding a property, contacting an agent, and scheduling a viewing should take minutes — not days of phone calls and email chains.
🔒
Data Privacy
Your personal data is never sold. We use it only to improve your property search experience, and you can delete your account at any time.
