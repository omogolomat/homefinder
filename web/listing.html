Property Details

This stunning executive villa sits within the prestigious Phakalane Golf Estate — one of Gaborone's most sought-after security estates. Built to an impeccable finish, the home offers 5 bedrooms, 4 bathrooms, and a sprawling open-plan living and dining area that flows seamlessly onto a covered patio and sparkling private pool.

The chef's kitchen is equipped with imported cabinetry, granite countertops, and a large island. The master suite occupies the entire upper level with en-suite bathroom, walk-in wardrobe, and private balcony overlooking the garden. Three further bedrooms are located on the ground floor, one of which has a private entrance ideal for extended family or domestic staff.

The double automated garage leads directly into the home. The estate provides 24-hour security, access control, and beautifully maintained communal areas. Located minutes from Phakalane Hotel, top schools, and the new Riverwalk Extension mall.
