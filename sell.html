Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I sell my own property without being an agent?
HomeFinder requires all property listings to be posted by a verified, REIB-licensed agent. However, if you own a property and want to sell, you can use the "Find an Agent" feature to connect with a top-rated agent who will list and manage the sale on your behalf. Many agents offer this service with a commission-based fee — typically 3–5% of the sale price in Botswana.
Do I need to be logged in to browse properties?
No — browsing and searching properties is completely free and open to everyone, with no account needed. You only need an account to save favourites, send messages to agents, or schedule viewings. Agents can also browse as regular users when not logged in.
How do I get verified as an agent?
After registering as an agent, upload your REIB (Real Estate Institute of Botswana) licence number, a copy of your ID, and your agency affiliation. Our team manually reviews submissions within 24 hours. Once verified, you receive the Verified Agent badge on all your listings and profile.
How do I pay for a featured listing?
Featured listings can be paid for via Orange Money, Smega, or bank transfer. Once payment is confirmed, your listing is boosted to featured placement immediately. We'll send you a receipt and invoice to your registered email address.
Can an agent also browse the site as a buyer?
Yes. Any agent can browse HomeFinder as a regular visitor without logging in — all listings and search features are publicly accessible. When logged in as an agent, you have access to both buyer features (save favourites, message other agents) AND all agent tools (post listings, manage enquiries, analytics dashboard).
